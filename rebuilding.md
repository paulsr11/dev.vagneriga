### **2. Section: How Riga made Wagner**
*   **Headline (H2):** How Riga made Wagner
*   **Numbered List:**
    1. In August 1837, Wagner arrived in Riga as chief conductor of the City Theatre. He was 24, unknown, and in debt.
    2. Here he encountered ideas that would define opera: the steep Greek-style seating, the darkened hall, the deep orchestra pit — all first realised in this building. He took every one of them to Bayreuth.
    3. In Riga he began composing *Rienzi* — his breakthrough work. When he fled the city in 1839, pursued by creditors, a violent storm in the Baltic gave him the vision for *The Flying Dutchman*.

### **6. Section: How we got here**
*   **Headline (H2):** How we got here
*   **Sub-headline:** From structural failure to active construction — a project eleven years in the making.
*   **Timeline Items:**
    *   **2025:** Venice Architecture Biennale (Scale model exhibited. "No Doubt About It.")
    *   **Oct 2024:** Foundation reinforcement begins **[ACTIVE]** (Piling works underway. Structural reinforcement.)
    *   **Apr 2024:** Demolition complete (Historic elements saved. Technical design finalised.)
    *   **May 2023:** Foundation stone laid (Wagner's 210th birthday. German parliamentary delegation present.)
    *   **Oct 2022:** €5M contract signed with Germany (Full agreement confirmed.)
    *   **Aug 2021:** First contract signed (€200K. Planning phase begins.)
    *   **Oct 2020:** Building handed over by Latvian state (Project officially begins.)
    *   **2014:** Wagner Society founded (Rescue mission starts.)
    *   **2007:** Building closes (Concerts stop. Structural failure.)
